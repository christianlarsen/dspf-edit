## Reporting a problem

If something isn't working as expected, please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/christianlarsen/dspf-edit/issues). Include:

- The DSPF-edit version (**Extensions** view → DSPF-edit → gear icon → the version shown, or check the [Marketplace listing](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=ChristianLarsen.dspf-edit)).
- A short DDS snippet that reproduces the issue, if possible.
- What you expected versus what actually happened — ideally compared against real **STRSDA** behavior, since that's what DSPF-edit aims to match.

## Feature requests

Feature requests are also welcome as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/christianlarsen/dspf-edit/issues).

## Enjoying DSPF-edit?

If it's saving you trips to SEU or STRSDA, consider [leaving a rating on the Marketplace](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=ChristianLarsen.dspf-edit&ssr=false#review-details) — it takes 10 seconds and is the single biggest thing that helps other IBM i developers find it.
